Values & Faith

I'm a Christian who attends services regularly. I'm imperfect, human, and still learning. I'm open to your faith background as long as Christ is recognized as Messiah.

Career & Passions

My professional life was extraordinary:

  • Short‑listed for astronaut selection
  • Holder of multiple FAA licenses (including commercial), though I never used the commercial license because the airline lifestyle didn’t appeal to me

I've traveled to 40+ countries, all seven continents, and even spent a week at the South Pole. Adventure is part of my nature.

Personal Life

My marriage lasted nearly 40 years. It was not fulfilling for either of us, but we honored our commitment. We raised two children; I remain close to one and estranged from the other. I have three granddaughters, though only one relationship is active.

Later in life, I adopted an adult daughter who had been abandoned by her father. We formed a genuine father‑daughter bond when she was young, and I helped her through school and life. She’s now 26, living far away, and still very much part of my heart. I do not tolerate insinuations about that relationship.

Friendships & Social Life
