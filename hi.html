Values & Faith

I'm a traditional man born in the 1950s — not in the sense of dominance, but in the sense of honor, respect, responsibility, and mutual strength. I believe in stepping up as a leader when the situation calls for it.

I'm a Christian who attends services regularly. I'm imperfect, human, and still learning. I'm open to your faith background as long as Christ is recognized as Messiah.

Career & Passions

My professional life was extraordinary:

  • NASA engineer/manager for ~25 years
  • USAF officer and fighter pilot for ~30 years (most simultaneously in the Reserves)
  • Commander of USAF forces at Kandahar & NATO Senior Airfield Authority
  • Short‑listed for astronaut selection
  • Holder of multiple FAA licenses (including commercial), though I never used the commercial license because the airline lifestyle didn’t appeal to me

I've traveled to 40+ countries, all seven continents, and even spent a week at the South Pole. Adventure is part of my nature.

UT Austin Recognition

Personal Life

My marriage lasted nearly 40 years. It ended in 2016. It was not fulfilling for either of us, but we honored our commitment. We raised two children; I remain close to one and estranged from the other. I have three granddaughters, though only one relationship is active due to the estrangement from one daughter.

Later in life, I adopted an adult daughter who had been abandoned by her father as a child. We formed a genuine father‑daughter bond when she was 12, and I helped her through school and life. She’s now 26, living far away, and still very much part of my heart. Some see it as odd. I followed my heart to help her get an education. I do not tolerate insinuations about that relationship.

Friendships & Social Life

I connect more easily with women than men — perhaps because men often compete with each other. I have female friends, including hiking partners, and I value those friendships. Jealousy has been an issue in past relationships, so I’m upfront about it.

Health & Lifestyle

My interests include:

  • Making my companion feel appreciated and loved - I'm a pleaser

Relationships & Intentions

I'm a one‑woman man. I can only invest deeply in one emotional and physical relationship at a time. I enjoy friendly banter — Texas Friendly — some might think I'm flirting but I'm just being friendly. Let there be no confusion - I'm 100% loyal and committed when I choose someone and they choose me back.

I'm a somewhat reformed romantic: I don’t fall fast anymore - but otherwise I'm a romantic. I want connection to grow naturally through shared values, friendship, and aligned expectations. Ultimately, I'm seeking a lifelong companion — ideally a wife.

Life Challenges & Growth

After returning from Afghanistan, my marriage dissolved and I faced difficult emotional terrain. I was later diagnosed with moderate PTSD and have been in counseling for several years. I'm grounded, stable, and generally well‑behaved these days :).
