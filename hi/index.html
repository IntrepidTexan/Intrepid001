Hello — and Welcome

An invitation to see whether our stories resonate.

If you're reading this, something about you caught my attention. Attraction may begin with the physical, but enduring connection comes from shared values, common interests, compatible goals, mutual respect, and that rare spark of chemistry. This page is hopefully a comfortable way for you to learn a little about me and decide whether you'd like to continue the conversation.

Values & Faith

I'm a traditional man — not in the sense of dominance, but in the sense of honor, respect, responsibility, and mutual strength. I practice equality in relationships with a tweak — if one party has superior skills or knowledge in a particular area, they should take the lead.

I'm a Christian who attends services regularly. I'm imperfect, human, and still growing and learning from my mistakes. Romans is my favorite book of the Bible. I'm open to your faith background so long as Jesus Christ is acknowledged as Messiah. I'll respect our differences of opinion and not try to change you. Of course, I expect the same.

Health & Lifestyle

My health is excellent, and my energy level is more than excellent :)) My spiritual, mental, financial, and emotional health are fine too; I'm truly blessed in so many ways. I don’t seek a nurse, purse, cook (though that would be nice :), or maid — just a partner who values me for my heart, mind, and soul rather than my wallet.

My interests include:

  • Long-distance hiking (eight Camino de Santiago trails so far)
  • DIY projects and home repairs - yes, I'm an engineer and can fix almost anything
  • Travel and adventure
  • Soaring / glider flying
  • Swing and two-step dancing
  • Quiet evenings, movies, snuggles, and reading together
  • Making my companion feel appreciated and loved — I'm a pleaser

Relationships & Intentions

I'm a one-woman man even though I infrequently enjoy friendly banter — some might say I'm flirting, but I'm just being Texas Friendly :).

That said, I have heritage female friends so seek a trusting woman who is secure in her skin. Please don't expect me to shed friendships; I've lost too many friends and comrades to war and aviation disasters. I won't now voluntarily sluff any more; I just can't — and hope you understand the mentality of a patriot. This is reciprocal of course.

Let there be no confusion — I'm 100% loyal and committed if/when we become exclusive. That doesn't mean we both can't have friends of all persuasions.

I'm a somewhat reformed romantic in that I work hard to not fall in love quickly. I much prefer the connection grow naturally through shared interests and values, a common faith, friendship, and aligned expectations. Ultimately, I'm seeking a lifelong companion — a wife.

Personal Life

My marriage lasted 35+ years but ended a decade ago after a few years separated. It was not entirely fulfilling for either of us as there were significant mismatches. We just married too young and for the wrong reasons. But we honored our commitment and raised two daughters; I remain close to one but am estranged from the other. I have three granddaughters, though only one relationship is active due to the estrangement.

I spent 25 years at NASA and 31 years in the Air Force (much of it overlapped while in the Reserves). I voluntarily left NASA and returned to USAF active duty after 9/11. After returning from my last Afghanistan deployment in 2007, my marriage dissolved and I faced difficult emotional terrain. I was later diagnosed with moderate PTSD and have been in VA-provided combat veterans counseling for several years. I'm grounded, stable, and well-mannered these days :)

Later in life, well after my divorce, I adopted a young lady who had been abandoned by her father as a child. We formed a genuine father-daughter bond when she was 12, and I helped her through school and life. She’s now in her mid-20s and, though she lives far away, is still very much part of my heart. I adopted her at 22; some see it as honorable, some as odd, and others see it through a negative lense. I followed my heart and did what I thought was the right thing to do because she wanted a dad and asked me to fill that void — and I wanted to help her through life and get an education (VA benefits). I care for her honorably and do not tolerate insinuations about our relationship.

Friendships & Social Life

It is an unfortunate truth that men don't initially trust each other and are apt to compete. Hence, I seem to connect more easily with women than men, except for my military battle buddies who are spread around the country and world. I have female friends, primarily hiking partners from around the world, and I value those friendships. All are currently in committed relationships, but jealousy has been an issue in past relationships so I’m upfront about it. Hiking is an important part of my life, so they will continue to fill the role of long distance hiking partner until you are ready to assume that role.

Career & Passions

My professional career is behind me now, and I don’t define myself by old titles or accomplishments. Still, those experiences reveal something important about my character: I’m persistent, disciplined, curious, adventurous, honorable and willing to work through difficult challenges rather than give up. Above all I'm persistent and a stickler for detail (in my profession people died from lack of it).

My professional life included some extraordinary experiences:

  • NASA engineer/manager for ~25 years (concurrent with my USAF career)
  • USAF senior officer and fighter pilot for ~30 years
  • Commander of USAF forces at Kandahar and NATO Senior Airfield Authority
  • Area Manager for US installations in Antarctica
  • Short-listed for astronaut selection until medically disqualified
  • Holder of multiple FAA pilot licenses, including commercial

I've traveled to more than 40 countries, all seven continents, and even spent a week at the South Pole. Adventure is part of my nature. I can regail you with stories ... but they'll probably bore you. :)
